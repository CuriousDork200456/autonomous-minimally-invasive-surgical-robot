02 / 08 — Problem Statement
The Gap We're Closing

Laparoscopic Surgery
Has Real Limits

🎯

Restricted Dexterity

Instruments enter through fixed port sites, eliminating natural wrist rotation and limiting reach to a 2D pivoting motion inside the cavity.

👁️

No Depth Perception

Standard endoscopes provide 2D feeds. Surgeons lose stereoscopic vision, increasing risk of accidental vessel damage by up to 3×.

🤝

Hand-Tremor Amplification

At the end-effector, natural physiological tremor (<1 mm) is amplified 3–5× by long instrument shafts — critical in microsurgery.

The result: Longer procedures, greater cognitive load on surgeons, higher complication rates in complex soft-tissue work — and no scalable autonomous safety net when surgeons approach critical structures.

Key Innovations

Semi-Autonomous Safety

ML module detects critical vessels and automatically overrides surgeon commands to prevent collision — surgeon stays in the loop supervisorily.

Sub-Millimetre Haptic Loop

Force-torque feedback from wrist sensor rendered back to surgeon's haptic gloves at <2 ms latency. Feel the tissue.

Redundant 7-DOF Kinematics

One extra DOF beyond minimum enables singularity avoidance and obstacle-free manoeuvring inside the abdominal cavity.

Cascaded Control Loop

An outer Cartesian admittance loop handles tissue-contact compliance; an inner computed-torque PD loop tracks joint trajectories in real time.

τ = M(q)q̈ + C(q,q̇)q̇ + G(q) + JᵀFₑₓₜ
Δq = Jᵀ(JJᵀ + λ²I)⁻¹ · Δx
T₀₇ = T₀₁ · T₁₂ · ... · T₆₇

The DLS Jacobian (λ = 0.01) ensures robustness near singularities, with Newton-Euler recursive dynamics computed at 1 kHz for stable force-torque control.
